# Canva + GitHub + lippytmai.getbizfunds.com Brand Money Engine

Purpose: connect Canva creative production, GitHub project control, and the lippytmai.getbizfunds.com website into one repeatable Business of Businesses growth system.

## Core outcome

Use Canva as the fast creative factory, GitHub as the control tower/version-control system, and lippytmai.getbizfunds.com as the public money-making website for offers, lead capture, affiliate links, funding services, AI bots, and project/platform promotion.

## Brand positioning

**Ready for Anything Business of Businesses — from startup to seasoned business owner.**

The brand should present lippytmai.getbizfunds.com as a practical AI-powered business hub that helps people:

- Find funding and business capital pathways.
- Build startup systems, LLC/trust/tax-readiness workflows, and automation foundations.
- Use AI bots, ManyChat, BotBuilders, Replit, GitHub, Canva, and website funnels together.
- Turn creative content into leads, affiliate income, consulting opportunities, product sales, and long-term platform value.

## Canva role: creative innovation factory

Create reusable Canva brand kits and templates for:

1. Website hero banners for lippytmai.getbizfunds.com.
2. Facebook/Instagram posts and reels covers.
3. Affiliate offer graphics for BotBuilders, ManyChat, Twin.so, Famous.ai, MyClaw/OpenClaw-style tools, and future AI platforms.
4. Ebook covers and lead magnet PDFs.
5. Pitch deck slides for funding, partnership, and investor conversations.
6. Short-form ad creatives for business funding, AI automation, and Business of Businesses offers.
7. T-shirt/hoodie merchandise concepts for lippytm.ai, CryptoClockworks, CryptoCyborg, CryptoCyberpunk, and related brand worlds.
8. One-page service menus and pricing sheets.
9. Testimonial/social proof cards.
10. QR-code cards that send traffic to lippytmai.getbizfunds.com, affiliate offers, chatbots, and GitHub project pages.
