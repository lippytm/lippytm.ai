## Responsible Messaging Notes

- Do not promise loan approval.
- Do not promise tax outcomes.
- Use “connect,” “support,” “help prepare,” “explore,” and “qualify.”
- Add tax disclaimer: educational/business-development support, not tax, legal, or financial advice unless provided by licensed professionals.
- Use AI as a business assistant, not as a replacement for licensed legal/tax/financial professionals.
