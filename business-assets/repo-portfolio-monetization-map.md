## Repo Hygiene Checklist for Making Money
Every monetizable repo should have:
- One-sentence customer outcome at the top of README.
- “Who this is for” section.
- “How it makes money / business use case” section.
- Install/run or demo instructions.
- Screenshots or a short demo link when possible.
- Clear CTA to getbizfunds, zo.computer, or a bot/automation offer.
- License and basic security notes.
- GitHub topics for discoverability.
- Issue templates for feature requests, leads, and integrations.
