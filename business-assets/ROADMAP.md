# lippytm.ai Business Asset Roadmap

## Role

`lippytm.ai` is the main Business of Businesses control hub connecting Canva, ChatGPT, GitHub, website funnels, CRM, AI bots, affiliate tools, and repository portfolio systems.

## Primary CTA

**Explore the Roadmap**

## Website destination

`/roadmap#explore`

## CRM tag

`Roadmap / Business Systems`

## Roadmap phases

### Phase 1: Organize the Hub

Document the core system in GitHub and keep GitHub as the source of truth.

### Phase 2: Create Canva Portfolio Cards

Create the first `lippytm.ai Control Hub` card, then duplicate it across priority repositories.

### Phase 3: Publish Website Roadmap Sections

Connect the Canva card to `/roadmap#explore` and give visitors a simple path to understand the system.

### Phase 4: Add CRM Tracking

Track roadmap interest, checklist downloads, bot workflow requests, and business systems review requests.

### Phase 5: Launch Social Campaigns

Publish the card and roadmap story across Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.

### Phase 6: Monetize the System

Route interested visitors to business systems reviews, bot workflows, funding-readiness checklists, affiliate tools, and consulting offers.

### Phase 7: Expand Across All Repositories

Repeat the business asset package across all lippytm repositories.

## Best practices

- Keep the public message simple.
- Use deeper pages for larger creative and technical visions.
- Track every CTA and CRM tag.
- Make every repository explain one useful outcome.
- Avoid guaranteed funding, income, approval, tax, legal, or investment claims.
