## What to review weekly

- Which platform produced the most clicks?
- Which post produced the most replies?
- Did anyone ask about business systems, bots, funding readiness, or Canva/GitHub help?
- Which CTA should be tested next?
- Does the website roadmap section need to be simplified?

## Improvement prompt

```text
Review this lippytm.ai performance log and suggest improvements to the headline, CTA, Canva card, website section, CRM follow-up, and next social posts. Goal: increase qualified roadmap interest and business systems review requests.
```

## Best practices

- Track weekly, even if numbers are small.
- Improve based on clicks and replies, not guesses.
- Reuse the best-performing caption as website copy.
- Reuse the best-performing visual as the master template.
