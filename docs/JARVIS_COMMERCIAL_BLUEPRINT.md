# Jarvis Commercial Blueprint

**Purpose:** Turn the repository portfolio into a narrow, testable path to first customer revenue. This is an operating proposal, not a claim that a SaaS product, payment flow, or integration is live.

## Position and first paid offer

Position the first offer as **AI-assisted lead response and appointment workflow setup** for one small-business customer at a time. The initial outcome is a reliably managed process: capture an inquiry, collect consent and key qualification details, prepare a timely follow-up, and route an appointment request to the customer's chosen calendar or human. Require human approval for every outbound message and appointment create/change/cancel action throughout the pilot, including after channel verification. A later change in autonomy needs a separately approved scope and safety validation.

Choose one niche after discovery; do not market simultaneously to clinics, real estate, ecommerce, agencies, and all local businesses. Start with one capture channel, one follow-up channel, and one booking destination. Integration count is capped at three for the pilot; count storage, middleware, and model APIs when accessed automatically, as defined in the [First Customer Package](JARVIS_FIRST_CUSTOMER_PACKAGE.md).

### Product ladder

| Stage | Product | Commercial treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Free checklist or sample from approved educational material | Opt-in lead magnet; state what follow-up the subscriber will receive |
| 2 | Ebook, audiobook, or videobook | Standalone digital product after content, rights, accessibility, and checkout are verified |
| 3 | Paid workflow discovery/setup pilot | First offer: scoped implementation and handoff; use a written statement of work |
| 4 | Ongoing workflow support/optimization | Recurring service only after support capacity, scope, and pricing are defined |
| 5 | Productized multi-tenant software, agency, or white-label offering | Later stage; requires demonstrated demand, security, support, billing, and operational readiness |

### Pricing

Do not publish a specific price until costs, scope, tax/refund handling, and willingness to pay are validated. Use visible placeholders in drafts: `[pilot setup fee]`, `[monthly support fee]`, `[digital product price]`, currency `[currency]`, and term `[term]`. Quote a fixed price only against an approved scope and written exclusions. No income, lead-volume, conversion, or business-result outcome is guaranteed.

## Customer journey

1. **Discover:** relevant educational content, referral, or Hostinger funnel page.
2. **Understand:** clear description of the narrow lead workflow, supported channels, limitations, privacy notice, and affiliate disclosure where applicable.
3. **Qualify:** prospect opts in and answers discovery questions; no sensitive data is requested in a public lead form.
4. **Review:** human checks fit, integration availability, data handling, and scope; decline unsupported or unsafe requests.
5. **Agree:** written scope, placeholder-to-approved pricing, permissions, data retention, and success measures.
6. **Onboard:** customer supplies authorized access through the provider's secure sharing method, not source control or chat; test with non-production data.
7. **Pilot:** use test cases, human approval, error monitoring, and fallback to customer staff.
8. **Evaluate:** report measured workflow outcomes, incidents, feedback, and costs; agree to continue, change, or stop.

## Revenue streams and funnel

- **Setup/service revenue:** scoped discovery, configuration, training, and handoff. Available only when delivery capacity and scope are confirmed.
- **Recurring support:** optional monitoring, content/prompt maintenance, or workflow review under a defined service agreement.
- **Digital media:** ebook, audiobook, and videobook editions; publish each only after the pipeline in [Content Product Pipeline](JARVIS_CONTENT_PRODUCT_PIPELINE.md).
- **Affiliate referrals:** planned Hostinger funnel for relevant hosting/domain/site education. A commission is not guaranteed and does not determine a recommendation.
- **Later product revenue:** subscription, marketplace, or white-label models remain hypotheses until built, tested, and supported.

### Hostinger affiliate funnel

Planned path: educational hosting/site setup content → a relevant Hostinger link → optional Jarvis workflow consultation or product CTA. Keep the Hostinger recommendation separate from the Jarvis service so readers can distinguish editorial advice from a commercial referral.

Place a clear, conspicuous disclosure beside or before the affiliate link, in plain language—for example: **“Disclosure: I may earn a commission if you purchase through this link, at no additional cost to you.”** Confirm the actual affiliate terms and required wording before publishing; disclose material connections in each relevant format (page, email, video/audio description). Do not imply Hostinger endorses Jarvis or that buying hosting guarantees business results. If no verified affiliate relationship/link is active, do not present it as active.

## Digital-media flywheel

Approved source material → one useful free excerpt/checklist → opt-in audience → complete ebook → adapted audiobook/videobook → educational promotion → one relevant CTA per asset → qualified inquiry → pilot feedback → improved content. Map each chapter/episode to a customer problem and a CTA, not an unsupported product capability. Track asset, source version, rights status, publication status, CTA, and attributable clicks/leads.

## Jarvis roles and model collaboration

- **AI Jarvis Assistant — Engineering Manager:** proposes priorities, issues, architecture, test/release checklists, and risk reports; cannot merge, deploy, spend, or change access without human approval.
- **AI Jarvis Assistant — Communications Manager:** drafts audience research briefs, campaign assets, affiliate copy, media calendars, and support responses; cannot send external messages, make unapproved claims, or commit a customer-facing price without human approval.
- Use ChatGPT for structured planning/orchestration, Claude for long-form analysis and drafting, Gemini for multimodal/creative alternatives, and Perplexity for current external research with citations. A human reconciles conflicting outputs and verifies sources before decisions or publication. See [Operational Runbook](JARVIS_OPERATIONAL_RUNBOOK.md).

## Ethical and compliance boundaries

- Obtain appropriate consent and honor opt-out, access, correction, and deletion requests under applicable requirements; collect only needed data and set a retention period before launch.
- Keep credentials out of documents, prompts, logs, and repositories. Use least-privilege access and approved secure credential exchange.
- Disclose affiliate relationships clearly; substantiate product, performance, pricing, and testimonial claims.
- Do not promise revenue, income, funding, lead volume, appointment volume, uptime, legal compliance, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not provide regulated legal, financial, medical, or tax advice. Route those requests to a qualified professional.
- Identify AI-assisted interactions where appropriate; offer a visible human handoff and a reliable non-AI fallback.
- Confirm permissions to reproduce, narrate, translate, adapt, and distribute source content before making commercial editions.

## Commercial evidence and approval record

The existing [Monetization Strategies](../MONETIZATION.md) remains the wider product/affiliate strategy, including Twin.so referrals, education, consulting, and later platform offerings. Its illustrative API tier prices, commissions, subscriber targets, and marketplace projections are not approved pilot pricing or achieved results. Hostinger remains a planned funnel; a referral URL or connected account alone does not verify program status, operational integration, payout, or attribution.

Before offering a delivery commitment, the Principal must approve the chosen niche/customer fit, exact deliverable and manual steps, final price/payment/refund terms, third-party cost cap, capacity/support hours, data/consent terms, acceptance measures, and permitted public claims. Record owner, decision, date, supporting evidence, and unresolved conditions in a private decision log. Product rights/checkout and each affiliate relationship/disclosure remain separate gates; do not make buying an affiliate product a condition of the pilot.

Current repository evidence supports preparation, scoping, and synthetic rehearsal. Live workflow delivery is conditional on the named-provider and manual-process evidence in the [First Customer Package](JARVIS_FIRST_CUSTOMER_PACKAGE.md). Discovery/scoping may be discussed with accurate limitations; do not sell unverified automation as an available service.

## Launch gate

Proceed to a paid pilot only when a real prospect has accepted a written scope; data/consent handling is documented; each selected connector has a successful test and fallback; approval and handoff work; pricing and support limits are explicit; and no critical/high-severity test or security issue is unresolved. Payment/billing is a separate implementation gate, not assumed by this blueprint.
