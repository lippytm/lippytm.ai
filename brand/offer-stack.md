# Business of Businesses Offer Stack

This offer stack turns lippytmai.getbizfunds.com, lippytm.ai, Canva, GitHub, BotBuilders, ManyChat, Twin.so, and future AI tools into a practical money-making brand system.

## Main brand promise

**Ready for Anything Business of Businesses — from startup to seasoned business owner.**

The brand helps people organize business funding, AI automation, creative marketing, digital products, affiliate tools, and long-term project systems.

## Best practices

- Sell outcomes, not tools.
- Make every offer connect to one next step.
- Use disclaimers: no guaranteed funding, income, approval, or investment result.
- Track every lead source.
- Reuse one idea across website, Canva, social, email, chatbot, and GitHub.
- Keep the first paid offer simple enough to deliver quickly.
