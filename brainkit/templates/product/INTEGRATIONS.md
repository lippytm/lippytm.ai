# Product Lane Integrations Template

## Common Connections
- receives standards from hub lane
- hands off complex tasks to swarm lane
- may route premium or paid paths into commerce lane

## Emits
- user interaction events
- task handoff events
- escalation events

## Receives
- service status updates
- assistant support context
- premium access signals

## Boundary Rule
Keep the user-facing surface simple and move deeper routing into swarm and control lanes.
