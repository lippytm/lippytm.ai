# Synthetic Intelligence Model

This document defines how **Synthetic Intelligence (SI)** should be layered into the lippytm ecosystem.

The purpose is to expand beyond isolated AI tools into a coordinated intelligence fabric that can reason, route, adapt, and improve across repositories, products, and business systems.

---

## Definition

### Artificial Intelligence
Focused model capability for generating, classifying, predicting, or assisting.

### Synthetic Intelligence
A system-level intelligence layer created by combining:

- multiple AI agents
- rules and policies
- telemetry and feedback
- memory summaries
- role specialization
- orchestration and routing
- cross-repo coordination

Synthetic Intelligence is not just one model. It is the **designed cooperation** of many intelligence components.

### Super Synthetic Intelligence
A higher-order orchestration layer that can:

- manage multiple SI clusters
- coordinate cross-lane missions
- govern self-improvement loops
- optimize value, quality, and control at fleet scale

---

## SI Layers

### Layer 1 — Model Intelligence
LLMs, classifiers, analyzers, and task-specific AI functions.

### Layer 2 — Agent Intelligence
Role-based agents such as architect, builder, tester, monetization, and documentation agents.

### Layer 3 — Swarm Intelligence
Coordinated groups of agents that share tasks, routing, and escalation.

### Layer 4 — Synthetic Intelligence
A managed system that combines swarms, policies, memory, telemetry, and repo coordination.

### Layer 5 — Super Synthetic Intelligence
A fleet-scale governing and optimization layer that supervises many SI systems across the ecosystem.

---

## SI Design Goals

The SI layer should improve:

1. coordination
2. reuse
3. decision quality
4. adaptive routing
5. lane-aware execution
6. monetization alignment
7. quality and recovery
8. scalable manufacturing of new systems

---

## Core SI Capabilities

- repo-aware reasoning
- task decomposition
- cross-agent handoff
- lane-aware routing
- policy-aware action limits
- failure recovery and replay
- memory summarization
- telemetry-driven improvement
- business-value awareness

---

## Super SI Capabilities

- mission-level planning across repos
- multi-swarm orchestration
- promotion recommendations from lab to product to production
- quality and monetization optimization loops
- cross-lane conflict detection
- manufacturing-template propagation
- self-coding proposals with governance boundaries

---

## Lane-Aware SI

SI should not erase lane separation.

Instead it should understand lanes and work differently inside each:

- `hub` → standards and propagation intelligence
- `control` → orchestration and governance intelligence
- `swarm` → routing and execution intelligence
- `revenue` → conversion and offer intelligence
- `product` → interface and support intelligence
- `commerce` → payment and access intelligence
- `knowledge` → education and content intelligence
- `lab` → experimentation and signal discovery

---

## Rule of thumb

AI becomes Synthetic Intelligence when many specialized intelligence functions are deliberately connected into one governed, adaptive operating system. It becomes Super Synthetic Intelligence when that operating system can supervise and improve many such systems at fleet scale.
