# Portfolio Revenue Growth Cycle

This workflow explains how to improve revenue across the portfolio using awareness, scorecards, CRM signals, money-first order, and bounded missions.

## Cycle

1. review revenue and CRM signals
2. review repo awareness and scorecards
3. rank money-adjacent repos by urgency and value
4. generate bounded missions for clarity, routing, activation, or follow-up
5. govern and execute changes
6. measure conversion, activation, and follow-up results
7. record lessons into templates and workflows

## Best Practices
- improve money-adjacent paths first
- keep CRM states and events explicit
- protect public-surface trust and checkout quality
- turn recurring revenue issues into template and policy upgrades
