# Service Value Chain

This workflow explains how value should move through the ecosystem from discovery to service delivery and follow-up.

## Value Chain

1. discovery and trust building
2. lead capture
3. qualification and routing
4. strategy or premium path selection
5. checkout and activation
6. delivery and support
7. follow-up and retention
8. product, content, or partner expansion

## Best Practices

- keep the next step clear at each stage
- preserve context across handoffs
- keep checkout and activation measurable
- use follow-up as a value-expansion path, not only as a reminder system
- connect service delivery back into knowledge, product, and partner growth where useful
