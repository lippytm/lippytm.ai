# Lane Mismatch Resolution

This workflow explains what to do when a repo, task, or planned change appears to belong in the wrong lane.

## Goal

Resolve lane mismatches without creating confusion, duplication, or uncontrolled cross-lane sprawl.

## Steps

1. identify the current lane
2. identify the intended work
3. decide whether the work belongs in the current lane
4. reroute, split, or promote as needed
5. document the decision

## Resolution Options
- reroute the work to the correct lane
- split the work into lane-specific parts
- promote the repo if maturity justifies it
- keep the current lane and document the boundary clearly

## Best Practices
- do not force every problem into the current repo
- use promotion rules before changing lane identity
- preserve public-surface clarity
- preserve creative and lab freedom without polluting production lanes
