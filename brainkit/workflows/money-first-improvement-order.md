# Money-First Improvement Order

This workflow explains how to prioritize improvements that most directly affect revenue, activation, and business value.

## Goal

Protect and improve the parts of the ecosystem that most directly influence money flow, trust, and service delivery.

## Priority Order

1. main revenue surfaces and offer pages
2. lead and strategy intake forms
3. checkout handoff and service activation paths
4. premium and partner routing paths
5. supporting product and assistant handoff flows
6. knowledge/media surfaces that feed trust and discovery
7. lab systems

## Best Practices

- fix high-risk money-adjacent paths first
- keep checkout and activation measurable
- use public-surface clarity as a business metric
- keep experiments from displacing urgent revenue work
