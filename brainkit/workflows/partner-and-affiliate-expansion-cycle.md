# Partner and Affiliate Expansion Cycle

This workflow explains how the portfolio can grow through partner and affiliate paths after core offers and activation flows are in place.

## Cycle

1. identify products or services suitable for partner promotion
2. define partner or affiliate fit
3. create routing and follow-up paths
4. track partner-origin leads and activation quality
5. improve based on signal quality and repeat value

## Best Practices

- choose partner paths that match real product/service value
- keep partner offers clear and auditable
- track partner-origin outcomes separately when useful
- use partner lessons to improve offer clarity and follow-up design
