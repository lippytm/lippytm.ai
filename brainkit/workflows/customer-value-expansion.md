# Customer Value Expansion

This workflow explains how to create more value from existing customer and service relationships across the portfolio.

## Cycle

1. confirm current service or product fit
2. identify the next useful path for the customer
3. route into assistant, swarm, knowledge, or partner paths when relevant
4. measure repeat engagement and expansion quality
5. improve based on conversion, activation, and retention signals

## Best Practices

- expand from real customer fit, not generic upsell pressure
- connect delivery quality to expansion timing
- keep next-step offers relevant and easy to understand
- use expansion lessons to improve products, workflows, and offer structure
