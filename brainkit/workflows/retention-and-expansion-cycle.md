# Retention and Expansion Cycle

This workflow explains how to improve value after activation or first sale.

## Cycle

1. confirm activation
2. record delivery context
3. schedule follow-up
4. identify next fit for product, service, or partner path
5. route into assistant, swarm, or knowledge paths when useful
6. measure repeat engagement
7. record lessons into workflows and templates

## Best Practices
- keep follow-up useful
- use real fit and timing for expansion paths
- connect delivery lessons back into the portfolio
