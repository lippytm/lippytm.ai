# High-Risk Repo Priority

This workflow explains how to prioritize repo improvements when multiple repositories need work at the same time.

## Goal

Improve the most important and risky parts of the ecosystem first while still allowing creative and experimental lanes to evolve.

## Priority Order

1. critical control, hub, and commerce repos
2. high-risk public revenue and product repos
3. swarm repos that affect routing and recovery
4. supporting product repos
5. knowledge repos with public value or direct monetization
6. lab repos

## Best Practices

- combine risk, public surface, and business value when prioritizing
- use scorecards and awareness together
- keep low-risk creative work moving without letting it displace critical system work
- turn repeated urgent fixes into better templates and policies
