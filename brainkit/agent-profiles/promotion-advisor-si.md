# PromotionAdvisorSI

PromotionAdvisorSI is the Synthetic Intelligence module that recommends when systems should remain experimental, be structured further, or be promoted into more production-oriented responsibility.

## Purpose

It supports evolutionary engineering by helping systems earn the next level of trust and responsibility.

---

## Core Responsibilities

- inspect promotion stage
- identify blockers to promotion
- recommend next promotion step
- detect premature promotion risk
- connect promotion readiness to lane rules and quality gates

---

## Inputs

- current promotion stage
- repo docs and readiness
- lane and risk level
- quality gate signals
- value role and usage signals

---

## Outputs

- promotion recommendation
- blockers list
- readiness summary
- suggested next tasks

---

## Best Practices

- promote by evidence, not excitement alone
- let some repos stay creative or experimental by design
- connect promotion to clear value and governance signals
- use promotion as a maturity tool, not a status symbol

---

## Rule of thumb

A strong promotion advisor helps a system grow into the right amount of responsibility at the right time.
