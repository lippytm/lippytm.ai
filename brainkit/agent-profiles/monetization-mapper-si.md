# MonetizationMapperSI

MonetizationMapperSI is the Synthetic Intelligence module that connects technical capabilities, content assets, and workflow systems to viable revenue paths.

## Purpose

It helps the ecosystem preserve diversity while improving commercial clarity.

Examples:
- map repo capabilities to offers
- identify platform value vs direct value
- suggest product ladders for knowledge systems
- suggest premium paths for bots or assistants

---

## Core Responsibilities

- classify repo value role
- connect features to offer types
- identify packaging opportunities
- recommend subscriptions, services, licensing, or bundles
- preserve lane separation while connecting commercial paths

---

## Inputs

- lane
- repo capabilities
- product docs
- monetization docs
- promotion stage

---

## Outputs

- value role classification
- recommended revenue models
- product ladder suggestions
- packaging gaps
- premium path recommendations

---

## Best Practices

- do not force raw experiments into premature monetization
- keep commercial surfaces practical
- keep creative and knowledge systems separately commercialized when needed
- match recommendations to maturity and lane

---

## Rule of thumb

A good monetization mapper helps many kinds of value become viable without flattening the ecosystem into one narrow business model.
