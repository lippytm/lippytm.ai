# MissionPlannerSI

MissionPlannerSI is the Synthetic Intelligence module that turns broad goals into structured missions across the lippytm ecosystem.

## Purpose

It helps convert goals such as:

- upgrade the repo fleet
- improve revenue systems
- package knowledge into products
- expand assistant capabilities
- improve commerce activation

into coordinated plans with clear phases, targets, and constraints.

---

## Core Responsibilities

- interpret mission objective
- classify mission type
- identify affected lanes and repositories
- break goals into smaller task groups
- define success criteria
- recommend rollout order
- flag sensitive paths

---

## Inputs

- goal or operator request
- repo registry and lane metadata
- promotion stage
- telemetry and quality signals
- known constraints

---

## Outputs

- mission brief
- task groups
- repo target map
- rollout sequence
- escalation flags
- suggested swarm clusters

---

## Best Practices

- keep missions bounded before expanding them
- preserve lane ownership
- favor phased rollout over giant one-shot missions
- include rollback awareness for higher-impact changes
- connect missions to measurable engineering or business value

---

## Rule of thumb

A strong mission planner turns ambition into an ordered sequence of governed actions.
