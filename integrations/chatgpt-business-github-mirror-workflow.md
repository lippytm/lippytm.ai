# ChatGPT Business + GitHub Mirror Workflow

## Purpose

Mirror creative work from ChatGPT Business into GitHub and Zo Computer so the Business of Businesses system can reuse, improve, publish, and monetize the best ideas.

This workflow is designed for LippytmAI, Business of Businesses, AI Business Funding Launch Kit, affiliate marketing, programming/blockchain/robotics education, and science-fiction educational entertainment campaigns.

## Source-of-truth order

1. **GitHub** — durable version-control source of truth for approved plans, copy, prompts, campaign assets, eBook drafts, and repo documentation.
2. **Zo Computer** — execution center for publishing, automation, files, Zo Space pages, agents, and services.
3. **ChatGPT Business** — creative origin point for ideation, variations, copywriting, sales copy, eBook drafting, and advertising angles.
4. **Claude** — refinement layer for long-context review, quality control, editorial tightening, code/document improvement, and alternative reasoning.
5. **Canva** — visual production layer for covers, thumbnails, social posts, PDFs, decks, QR flyers, and video title cards.

## Mirror process

### Step 1 — Capture ChatGPT Business output

When a strong ChatGPT Business idea, conversation, strategy, or draft is created, save it in one of these forms:

- Markdown export
- copied transcript
- prompt + output pair
- campaign brief
- eBook chapter draft
- sales copy batch
- Canva prompt batch

Recommended filename format:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-topic-source-chatgpt-business.md
```

### Step 2 — Save into Zo workspace

Place the file into the correct folder:

- `Records/Workflows/` for operating workflows
- `Campaigns/<campaign-name>/` for campaign assets
- `Articles/LippytmAI/` for articles
- `Ebooks/<ebook-name>/` for eBook drafts
- `Records/Plans/` for strategy and roadmap docs

### Step 3 — Improve with Zo + Claude-style review

Review for:

- clear audience
- clear offer
- clear CTA
- revenue logic
- practical next step
- compliance and disclaimers
- ability to publish as website, email, social, article, PDF, or eBook

### Step 4 — Commit approved version to GitHub

For the `lippytm.ai` repo, recommended destinations are:

```text
campaigns/
articles/
ebooks/
integrations/
business-assets/
operations/
docs/
```

Every GitHub commit should preserve the business purpose in the message, such as:

```text
Add AI funding launch kit ebook draft
Add ChatGPT Business mirror workflow
Add sales copy pack for funding campaign
```

### Step 5 — Publish or schedule

Publish useful outputs to one or more channels:

- Zo Space landing page
- Zo Space article
- GitHub markdown
- Canva design
- social post
- video script
- lead magnet PDF/eBook
- email sequence
- funding CTA page

## Best-practice operating rules

- One campaign should have one audience, one problem, one promise, and one CTA.
- Do not overload every page with every business idea.
- Preserve wild creative ideas, but convert the public offer into practical business language.
- Use the science-fiction wrapper to create attention, then move quickly into serious business value.
- Avoid guarantees of funding, income, approval, investment returns, or legal/tax outcomes.
- Use affiliate disclosures anywhere tools, programs, or partner offers may produce commissions.
- Keep eBooks modular so chapters can become articles, emails, social posts, landing pages, and Canva slides.

## Weekly workflow

1. Collect best ChatGPT Business outputs.
2. Choose one revenue campaign to improve.
3. Convert one idea into an article or landing page.
4. Convert one idea into eBook material.
5. Convert one idea into social/ad copy.
6. Save approved material to Zo Computer.
7. Commit to GitHub.
8. Publish the strongest public-facing asset.
9. Track what was published and what should be improved next.
