## Decision rules

### Repeat

Repeat content when:

- It gets replies.
- It gets saves/shares.
- It gets clicks.
- It generates leads.
- It explains the offer clearly.

### Improve

Improve content when:

- It gets views but no clicks.
- People ask confused questions.
- CTA is unclear.
- Design is hard to read.
- The offer is too broad.

### Stop

Stop or pause content when:

- It creates no engagement after repeated tests.
- It is too confusing.
- It requires too much explanation.
- It creates compliance risk.
- It does not connect to a revenue path.
