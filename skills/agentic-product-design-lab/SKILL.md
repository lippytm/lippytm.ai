# Agentic Product Design Lab

## Purpose

Turn agentic workflows and AI swarm ideas into viable, flexible, diverse, revenue-ready products and offers.

## Product design workflow

1. Define the buyer and urgent problem.
2. Choose product type: eBook, template, automation setup, service package, course, tool, SaaS prototype, affiliate hub, or membership.
3. Define the promise without overclaiming.
4. List deliverables and implementation steps.
5. Add proof assets: demo page, GitHub doc, screenshot, video, checklist, case-study-style example, or public workflow.
6. Add pricing options and upsells.
7. Add safety, disclosure, and support boundaries.
8. Create the first public asset and GitHub-backed source file.

## Design lenses

- Viability: can it generate leads or revenue soon?
- Diversity: can it serve multiple buyer types or industries?
- Flexibility: can it become a service, template, eBook, tool, or course?
- Defensibility: does it use unique LippytmAI assets, workflows, skills, or story worlds?
- Automation: can Zapier, GitHub, Zo, or agents reduce manual work?
- Safety: are claims, permissions, and data boundaries clear?

- Pricing options

- First build step
