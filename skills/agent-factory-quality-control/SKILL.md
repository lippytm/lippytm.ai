# Agent Factory Quality Control

## Purpose

Prevent low-quality, unsafe, vague, unprofitable, or unmanageable swarms from being mass-produced.

## Review checklist

Score each category 1-5:

1. Business viability: clear user, pain, offer, CTA, and revenue path.
2. Task clarity: narrow mission and measurable output.
3. Skill fit: correct skills selected and unnecessary complexity removed.
4. Safety: human approval gates for public, financial, legal, payment, external-message, lead/contact, destructive, and high-impact actions.
5. Interoperability: task/result packets, adapter contract, data boundaries, and logging are clear.
6. Quality: output is useful, specific, practical, and differentiated.
7. Maintainability: GitHub path, owner, version, rollback plan, and update loop are documented.
8. Productization: can become a template, lead magnet, service, affiliate funnel, SaaS/free tool, or eBook chapter.
9. Observability: success metrics, error notes, and review cadence are defined.
10. Brand fit: serious business with entertainment wrapper where appropriate.

## Decision rules

- Average 4.5+: production candidate.
- Average 4.0-4.49: pilot candidate with notes.
- Average 3.0-3.99: revise before pilot.
- Below 3.0: reject or simplify.

## Output required

Return:

- QC scorecard
- Approval status
- Required fixes
- Optional improvements
- Risk notes
- Productization notes
- Next action
