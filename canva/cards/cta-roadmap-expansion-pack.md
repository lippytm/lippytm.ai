## CTA strategy

A CTA should not be just a button. It should be the doorway into the next business action.

Each CTA needs:

- A clear promise.
- A low-friction next step.
- A landing section or page.
- A follow-up path.
- A tracking metric.
- A deeper offer behind it.

## CTA design best practices in Canva

### Button style

Use a strong but clean CTA button:

- Large enough to read on a phone.
- High contrast against background.
- Positioned in the lower third.
- Not hidden by graphics.
- Uses action language.

### CTA hierarchy

Every card should have only one primary CTA.

Bad:

```text
Explore / Buy / Learn / Contact / Join / Build / Download
```

Good:

```text
Explore the Roadmap
```

### CTA placement

Recommended card layout:

```text
Top: category badge
Middle: project headline + central visual
Lower middle: microcopy
Bottom: CTA button + footer
```
