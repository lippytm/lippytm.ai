# lippytmai.getbizfunds.com Homepage Wireframe

Purpose: turn lippytmai.getbizfunds.com into the public conversion hub for the Business of Businesses brand.

## Page goal

Convert visitors into:

- Funding-readiness leads.
- AI automation prospects.
- BotBuilders/ManyChat setup prospects.
- Affiliate offer clicks.
- Ebook/checklist downloads.
- Consulting or strategy call requests.

## Above-the-fold hero

### Headline

Ready for Anything Business of Businesses

### Subheadline

Funding, AI automation, creative marketing, and business systems from startup to seasoned business owner.

### Supporting copy

Build a practical business money engine with funding-readiness tools, AI bots, Canva creative assets, GitHub project control, affiliate opportunities, and website funnels that work together.

### Primary CTA

Get the Free Funding + AI Automation Checklist

### Secondary CTA

Explore the Business of Businesses System

### Visual direction

Use Canva hero banner: futuristic business finance, AI automation dashboard, entrepreneurs, digital network, clear CTA space.

## Section 2: What this helps you do

### Heading

Get Organized, Automated, Fundable, and Market-Ready

### Cards

#### Funding Readiness

Prepare your business information, documents, offers, and growth plan before looking for funding or capital pathways.

#### AI Automation

Use bots and workflows to capture leads, answer questions, follow up, and reduce repetitive work.

#### Creative Marketing

Use Canva templates, social content, PDFs, pitch decks, and landing pages to show value and build trust.

#### Business Systems

Connect website, GitHub, AI tools, ManyChat, BotBuilders, affiliate offers, and project roadmaps into one trackable system.
