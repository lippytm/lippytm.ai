## CTA

Explore the Roadmap

## Headline

lippytm.ai: The Control Hub for the Business of Businesses

## Copy

lippytm.ai connects the major parts of the ecosystem: Canva designs, ChatGPT workflows, GitHub repositories, website funnels, AI bots, CRM tracking, affiliate tools, education products, and platform concepts.

This roadmap is designed to turn every project into something useful: a campaign, an offer, a lead magnet, a portfolio card, a learning product, a bot flow, or a website section.

## Roadmap phases

### Phase 1: Organize the Hub

Create the central source of truth in GitHub and document the Business of Businesses system.

### Phase 2: Create Canva Portfolio Cards

Turn priority repositories into visual assets that explain the project and guide people to a CTA.

### Phase 3: Publish Website Sections

Connect each card to a page or section so visitors have somewhere useful to go.

### Phase 4: Launch Social Campaigns

Use Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn to introduce the portfolio, offers, and roadmap.

### Phase 5: Build Bot Workflows

Use Chatlippytm.ai.Bots, ManyChat, BotBuilders, and AI assistant workflows to capture and route interest.

### Phase 6: Add CRM Tracking

Track who clicked, what they wanted, what they downloaded, and what follow-up is needed.

### Phase 7: Expand Across All Repositories

Repeat the system across every repo: card, website copy, social pack, CTA, CRM tag, GitHub issue, and performance log.

## Bullets

- Canva turns the projects into visual assets.
- ChatGPT creates strategy, copy, prompts, and workflows.
- GitHub stores the plans and tracks execution.
- Website pages turn interest into next steps.
- CRM tracks leads and follow-up.
- Social media distributes the message.

# Section 3: Map Your Bot System

## Section ID

`#allbots-map`

## Connected Canva card

`AllBots.com`

## CTA

Map Your Bot System

## Headline

Map Your Bot System Before You Build More Bots

## Copy

AllBots.com is a bot management concept for organizing multiple bots by role. Every bot should have a job, a next step, and a measurable business outcome.

Instead of building random automations, map the system first.

## Bot roles

### Lead Bot

Captures new interest and asks the first qualifying questions.

### FAQ Bot

Answers repeat questions and reduces repetitive work.

### Sales Bot

Routes people to the right offer, product, service, or consultation path.

### Support Bot

Helps customers get basic information or route to support.

### Affiliate Bot

Recommends approved tools or partner offers with proper disclosure.

### Follow-Up Bot

Keeps the conversation moving after the first contact.

### Training Bot

Teaches users, customers, or team members how to use systems and workflows.

## AllBots system map

```text
Website Traffic
  -> Lead Bot
  -> FAQ Bot
  -> Sales Bot
  -> Support Bot
  -> Affiliate Bot
  -> Follow-Up Bot
  -> Training Bot
  -> CRM + GitHub Tracking
```

## Intake questions

1. How many bots or automations do you already use?
2. What does each bot do?
3. Which bot captures leads?
4. Which bot handles FAQs?
5. Which bot routes to offers?
6. Which bot follows up?
7. Which bot needs human handoff?
8. What result should each bot produce?
