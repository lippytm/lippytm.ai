Chooses the business objective, audience, revenue model, offer type, and success metric.

Creates Zo Space pages, GitHub docs, workflow files, prompt packs, templates, and lightweight tools.

Designs Zapier templates, lead routing, GitHub issue creation, CRM/spreadsheet logs, notifications, and follow-up paths.

Checks claims, disclosures, usability, safety, business logic, technical correctness, links, and compliance-sensitive language.

Defines permissions, approval gates, rollback plans, audit logs, and safe escalation paths.

## Innovation principles

- Build modular systems, not isolated assets.
- Use small safe loops before attempting autonomy.
- Turn every creative idea into a reusable workflow.
- Turn every workflow into a product candidate.
- Treat GitHub as the memory and audit trail.
- Treat ChatGPT Business as creative generation.
- Treat Canva as visual packaging.
- Treat Zapier as cross-app movement.
- Treat Zo Computer as execution, publishing, and automation.
- Treat Claude-style review as refinement, safety, and quality control.

## Viability checklist

Before expanding any agent or swarm, answer:

- What money path does this support?
- Who benefits from it?
- What exact asset will be created?
- Where will the source live?
- What public page or funnel will it support?
- What automation can safely improve it?
- What must remain human-approved?
- What can be reused later?

## Safety checklist

- No funding, income, legal, tax, medical, or investment guarantees.
- Use affiliate disclosures.
- Protect private information.
- Keep secrets out of files and prompts.
- Require approval before sending external messages.
- Keep rollback paths for pages, GitHub commits, and Zapier automations.
- Log major outputs in workspace and GitHub.
