# Data Value Calculator Launch Pack

## Core Hook

> Big Tech and AI companies may generate up to **$831,497** from one U.S. internet user’s data over a digital lifetime. What is *your* data worth?

This campaign points traffic to `website/data-value-calculator.html`, a static calculator inspired by the Web3 Foundation report, *The Hidden Price of Free: What Your Data Is Really Worth*.

## Positioning

The calculator is a simple viral front door for the lippytm.ai/Web3 data ownership story:

1. Make the invisible value of personal data visible.
2. Explain why “free” platforms are really data-for-service exchanges.
3. Move the conversation from outrage to ownership: wallets, consent receipts, data vaults, AI agent permissions, and user-controlled monetization.

## X / LinkedIn Thread Draft

1. Big Tech calls the internet “free.” A 2026 Web3 Foundation report puts a number on the trade: up to **$831,497** in lifetime commercial value from each U.S. internet user’s data.
2. That number includes more than ads: AI subscriptions, enterprise licensing, API access, data brokerage, marketplaces, recommendations, and AI-driven cost savings.
3. Your searches, prompts, purchases, location, messages, and behavior are not exhaust — they are raw material.
4. The problem is not that data creates value. The problem is that users usually get no visibility, bargaining power, or participation in the value chain.
5. Web3 data ownership means moving from invisible extraction to explicit permission: wallets, consent receipts, revocation, programmable access, and user-controlled monetization.
6. I built a calculator to make the invisible visible: “What is your data worth?”
7. Try it, share your estimate, and ask: if my data creates this much value, what should the next internet look like?
