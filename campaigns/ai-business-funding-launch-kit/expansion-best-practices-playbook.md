# AI Business Funding Launch Kit — Expansion Best Practices Playbook

## Purpose

Continue expanding the AI Business Funding Launch Kit with greater detail, depth, innovation, design quality, viability, diversity, flexibility, and best practices.

## Strategic direction

The campaign should operate as four connected assets:

1. **Revenue funnel** — lead capture and funding-related inquiries.
2. **Educational product** — eBook, checklist, training lessons, and practical business preparation.
3. **Automation system** — Zapier, GitHub issues, CRM/spreadsheet logging, follow-up, and reporting.
4. **Entertainment marketing universe** — Space Aliens, Time Machines, robots, programming, blockchain, and future-of-business storylines.

## Viability standards

A new asset is viable if it has:

- a clear target audience
- a clear business problem
- a practical solution
- a next step
- a monetization path
- a reusable format
- a compliance-safe message

## Diversity standards

Every major campaign should include a mix of:

- beginner-friendly education
- advanced strategy
- practical templates
- creative entertainment
- serious business authority
- short-form social content
- long-form search content
- GitHub documentation
- Canva-ready visuals
- Zapier-ready automations

## Flexibility standards

Each core idea should be reusable as:

- article
- eBook chapter
- checklist
- landing page
- video script
- social post
- email
- GitHub issue
- Canva prompt
- Zapier step

## Best-practice warnings

- Do not promise funding approval.
- Do not promise guaranteed income.
- Do not present educational material as legal, tax, or investment advice.
- Do not make the form too long at first touch.
- Do not mix too many CTAs on one page.
- Do not leave valuable ChatGPT Business work unmirrored.
- Do not publish affiliate recommendations without disclosure.
