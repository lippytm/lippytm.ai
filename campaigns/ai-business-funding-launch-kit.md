# AI Business Funding Launch Kit Campaign

Purpose: create the first practical cash-flow campaign for lippytmai.getbizfunds.com using Canva, GitHub, AI automation, website lead capture, affiliate links, and follow-up systems.

## Campaign promise

Help entrepreneurs get funding-ready, AI-ready, and market-ready with one practical starter kit.

## Target audience

- New business owners.
- Side-hustle builders.
- Existing small businesses.
- Affiliate marketers.
- Creators and service providers.
- People who need better organization before seeking funding or automation help.

## Main offer

**Free Funding + AI Automation Starter Checklist**

Lead magnet goal: capture email/contact leads and route them into the correct offer path.

## Paid follow-up offers

1. Startup Readiness Kit.
2. Business Growth Automation Kit.
3. AI Bot Builder Package.
4. Canva Brand Money Engine Pack.
5. Innovation Partner Deck + Roadmap.

## Funnel flow

1. Social post, reel, flyer, QR code, or affiliate/tool content sends visitor to lippytmai.getbizfunds.com.
2. Visitor downloads free checklist.
3. Form asks what they need most.
4. Follow-up message routes them to the right offer.
5. Bot or email sequence educates, qualifies, and invites them to take the next step.
6. Best prospects are invited to request a Business Systems Review.

## Best practices

- Keep the first CTA simple: free checklist.
- Do not overload the homepage with every project at once.
- Use the mythos/creative universe as brand depth, but keep the front-door business offer practical.
- Publish before perfecting; improve weekly.
- Use GitHub issues for tasks and campaign accountability.
- Use Canva templates so one idea becomes many assets.
- Add disclaimers to funding, affiliate, tax, legal, and income-related content.
