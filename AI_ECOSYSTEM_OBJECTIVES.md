# AI Ecosystem Objectives

This document defines the main objectives of the wider `lippytm.ai` AI development and education ecosystem.

## Purpose
Create a connected ecosystem for:
- AI development
- AI education
- teaching and training systems
- builder self-improvement
- repo-based learning
- experimentation and productization

## Primary Objectives
- help builders become better programmers
- help builders become better blockchain developers
- teach AI workflow design through practical repository systems
- turn experiments into repeatable lessons
- turn lessons into reusable educational applications
- support Linux-first and mixed-environment development paths
- strengthen documentation, reflection, and creative problem solving

## Ecosystem Roles
- public navigation and orientation
- control tower governance and standards
- internal AI operations
- AI coding and builder learning
- experimentation and sandboxing
- productization and reusable kits
- knowledge architecture and teaching loops

## Guiding Principle
Use repositories as connected environments for learning, building, teaching, reflecting, and improving AI systems over time.
